🔲 STEP 1: THE TACTICAL CHUNKING WARM-UP
 Time Allocation: Exactly 30 Minutes
 Platforms Allowed: Lichess Puzzles or Chess.com Custom Puzzles
 The Goal: Force your eyes to automatically track tactical lines without manual searching.
Detailed Execution Instructions
1. Navigate to ⁠Lichess.org/training/themes⁠ or go to Chess.com custom puzzles.
2. Crucial Rule: Do NOT use the default "Random Puzzle" button. You must click into the specific folder assigned for that day of the week.
3. Once inside the folder, solve puzzles continuously for 30 minutes.
4. The No-Click Policy: You are strictly forbidden from moving a piece on the screen until you have calculated the entire solution to the very end inside your head. Guessing the first move and "seeing what happens" completely destroys the training.
The Weekly Folder Schedule
 Monday Folder: Pins (Exploiting a piece that cannot move because a more valuable piece sits behind it).
 Tuesday Folder: Forks / Double Attacks (Landing a piece on a square that strikes two undefended targets at once).
 Wednesday Folder: Skewers (Hitting a high-value piece like a King or Queen, forcing it to step aside so you can steal the piece hiding behind it).
 Thursday Folder: Back-Rank Checkmates (Punishing a King trapped on the 8th/1st rank behind its own wall of defensive pawns).
 Friday Folder: Discovered Attacks (Moving a frontline piece out of the way to suddenly open up a laser-line attack from a hidden Rook or Bishop behind it).
 Saturday & Sunday Mode: Open Lichess Puzzle Streak. This is your exam. Run the streak until you fail 3 times. This tests your overall tactical speed across mixed themes.
🔲 STEP 2: THE ADAPTATION LIVE MATCHES
 Time Allocation: 1 Hour to 1 Hour and 15 Minutes
 The Goal: Transfer your morning puzzle patterns into live human gameplay while eliminating unforced errors.
Detailed Execution Instructions
1. Open the play dashboard and select the 15+10 Rapid time control (15 minutes baseline clock with a 10-second bonus added to your clock every time you make a move).
2. Strict Ban List: You are entirely banned from playing 1-minute (Bullet), 3-minute, or 5-minute (Blitz) chess. Fast time controls force your brain to rely on panicked, low-Elo instincts.
3. Queue up and play exactly Two Games per session.
The "Checks, Captures, Threats" (CCT) Mental Loop
The moment your opponent clicks their mouse and their move lands on the board, your brain must execute this exact internal dialogue before you even decide what piece you want to move:
1. Point 1: "Why did they go there? What did their piece just open up or uncover?"
2. Point 2: "Does their new position create a direct Check to my King, a Capture on my material, or a tactical Threat to my pieces?"
3. Point 3: "Look at my intended target square. If I put my piece there, is it completely safe, or am I walking directly into a free capture?"
🔲 STEP 3: THE COGNITIVE RECOVERY ANALYTICS
 Time Allocation: 10 Minutes (5 Minutes per live match)
 The Goal: Fix errors while your memory of the board's tension is still hot.
Detailed Execution Instructions
1. The exact millisecond your Rapid match finishes, click the Analysis button. Do NOT queue up for a third game.
2. Drag the game slider back to the opening, and slowly click through the moves.
3. Look closely at the visual evaluation bar on the side of the board. Find the exact move where the bar plummets in your opponent's favor.
4. The Verification Test: Before you click the "Show Best Move" button, stare at the board for 60 seconds. Force yourself to identify why your move was bad. Did you hang a free piece? Did you miss a hidden fork? Name your error out loud, then click the engine button to verify if your diagnostic was correct.

 Time Allocation: 30 Minutes
 The Goal: Achieve legendary-level execution by crushing an unbeatable engine that has been mathematically handicapped.
Detailed Execution Instructions
1. Go to Chess.com or ⁠Lichess.org/analysis⁠ and click on the Board Editor tool.
2. Clear every single piece off the board.
3. Manually place the pieces on the squares to match the exact structural layout assigned in the syllabus below.
4. Click "Practice vs Computer".
5. Slide the difficulty bar to the absolute maximum setting: Level 8 / Stockfish 16 / 3200+ Elo.
6. The Gatekeeper Rule: You cannot skip ahead in the calendar. You are locked into your current block until you can successfully checkmate this maximum-strength engine 5 times in a row. If you draw, get stalemated, or lose your material advantage, your counter resets back to 0.
